ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Akshat Vishwakarma.

Building intelligent systems. Designing meaningful experiences. Solving real-world problems.

I'm a third-year B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Sanjivani University. I enjoy turning complex ideas into practical products through machine learning, software engineering, and thoughtful design.

From developing AI-powered platforms like GeneGuard and SecureSupply to building solutions for real-world challenges, I love experimenting with technology, learning by building, and pushing beyond what I already know.

I've participated in 7+ national hackathons and an international hackathon, including a Top 35 finish at SunHacK International 2025. Alongside development, I work in graphic design and UI/UX, bringing a balance of technical thinking and visual creativity to everything I build.

Curious by nature. Builder by choice. Always learning.
